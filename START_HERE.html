The numbers behind the run.

Twelve replayable movement experiments. Start with case 01, then compare case 10 (marked receiver) and case 05 (stationary runner). Click a replay for playback, scrubbing, metric evidence and a spatial-change overlay.

All matches and players are synthetic. Tracking is 50 Hz; GIF/AVI previews are 5 fps. Area is model sensitivity, defender travel is association, and xG is unavailable. These experiments demonstrate the pipeline, not professional-match accuracy.
